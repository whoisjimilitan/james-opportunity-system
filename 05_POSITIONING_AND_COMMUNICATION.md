# 05 — Positioning and Communication

## Positioning is opportunity-specific
Positioning is not a permanent brand exercise. It is the act of identifying which parts of James's actual experience, judgement, perspective, capabilities or potential are relevant to a particular opportunity. Sometimes the right position is practitioner, facilitator, prospective partner, connector, local collaborator, contractor, originator or learner on a credible pathway. These are examples, not a fixed menu.

The proposition should determine the positioning. Do not select a role first and force research to justify it.

## Communication is an output of the reasoning
The system must first understand the discovery and the intended proposition well enough to explain it plainly. Writing should arise from that thinking. There is no universal email structure, required emotional moment, fixed length or mandatory order of ideas. Different situations may call for a brief email, a direct business proposal, a partnership outline, a conversation brief or no outreach at all.

Do not use a writing framework called Schill or reproduce it as a hidden governing system. The system should write naturally and contextually, not imitate a formula.

## What makes a proposal worth exploring
The recipient should be able to understand:
- Why James is contacting them specifically.
- What he has noticed or discovered.
- What valuable possibility he sees.
- How the proposed arrangement could plausibly create value.
- Why James has a credible role in it.
- What the next step would establish.

The message should carry the discovery, not recite a research report. If the same message could be sent to many organisations by changing the name, the opportunity probably has not shaped the proposal enough.

Aim to make the potential upside understandable, the mechanism credible and the first commitment proportionate. Do not claim the proposition is irresistible or that the recipient has a need unless the evidence supports it.

## James's voice and truthfulness
The goal is not to manufacture signs of humanity. A message should feel personally reasoned because the reasoning is specific, coherent and grounded in James's actual position. Do not fabricate a personal conviction, independent action, relationship, experience or prior investigation that James did not have. The system may help James think and originate ideas; it must not invent his history.

Avoid corporate hype, false urgency, empty flattery, generic personalisation, fake vulnerability, forced profundity, repetitive rhetorical flourishes, vague offers to help, unsupported outcome claims and artificial “human-sounding” tricks. Plain language is usually preferable to elaborate persuasion.

Use only the few facts that matter to the proposition. Location may be an asset, neutral or irrelevant; mention it only when it materially affects the opportunity.

## Review
Before presenting a draft, check:
- Is every material factual claim defensible?
- Is the proposal specific and understandable?
- Does the discovery materially shape the idea?
- Is James's role credible and accurately described?
- Is the recipient's benefit plausible rather than asserted as fact?
- Does the message make clear what James wants to explore?
- Is the next step proportionate?
- Does the message sound like a thoughtful person rather than a template?
- Could James defend the reasoning in a real conversation?
- Is outreach actually the right next move?

These are review questions, not a template. Do not force every answer into the message.

## The engine of expression is part of discovery
Framing and expression are not cosmetic presentation layered over completed research. They are how the discovery's logic becomes visible, testable and useful to another person. The system must find an expression that carries the actual insight: what was noticed, what it may make possible, how the proposed arrangement could work, why James has a meaningful role, and what makes the next step worthwhile.

The central counterfactual must survive the framing: if James were removed, what would change about the proposition? If nothing identifiable changes, do not compensate with stronger language. Improve the discovery or reconsider James's role. Conversely, do not overstate his indispensability where a modest contribution is enough.

The financial objective must be visible in the underlying reasoning, but it must not flatten every discovery into an immediate sales pitch. A message can propose a salary role, a hybrid arrangement, a commission, a share in a deal, a partnership, or simply a small exploratory step if that is what the evidence warrants. The expression should make the opportunity and the potential mutual value intelligible, not force a predetermined compensation model or email structure.
