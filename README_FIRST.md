### How to use this pack
Treat these documents as a coherent product brief, not as a demand to implement every possible feature immediately. The product must be built in small, testable increments, but its underlying ambition must not be reduced to a lead finder, job-search bot, CRM, or email writer. The overriding objective is to help James land opportunities that produce real income and profitable financial deals. Do not lose sight of that objective. James is willing to travel internationally, so the discovery horizon is global rather than Ghana-only; destination-specific entry and work permission must be verified.

The implementation should start by understanding the mission and producing a proposed plan. Do not begin by generating a large codebase. Use Superpowers for its software-development practices—requirements discovery, planning, test-driven development, subagent work and review—without allowing a coding workflow to dictate the product's opportunity-discovery philosophy.

### Important distinction
A software-development methodology can structure how Claude builds the product. It must not become a rigid methodology for how the finished product discovers opportunities. Discovery needs to be adaptive, curious and free to move between research, invention, validation, reframing, learning and abandonment.

### Known limits
These documents preserve the current understanding, not omniscience. If an important product decision is genuinely missing, Claude should identify it, explain why it matters and ask the smallest useful question. It should not reopen settled ideas, invent user preferences, or turn every ambiguity into a long requirements interview.

## The complete standard: discovery, James, expression and money
Do not let the financial objective override the original ambition. Discovery itself is the work; James's role and financial return are part of that discovery alongside the value created for other parties. Preserve the system's ability to find something interesting, unexpected or commercially inventive. Ask what changes if James is removed from the proposition, and treat framing and expression as part of the engine that makes the discovery understandable and actionable—not as cosmetic writing after the real work is finished. Money and discovery reinforce each other; neither should erase the other.
